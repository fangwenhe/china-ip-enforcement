# Opposition, Invalidation & Non-Use Cancellation — Detailed Flow (China)

> Detailed playbook for the three main administrative weapons against a squatted or conflicting Chinese trademark. Times are typical ranges; official notices govern your specific deadline.

## Decision tree — which weapon?

```
Squatter's mark status?
├─ Still in 3-month publication window
│  └─ OPPOSITION (异议) — cheapest, highest win rate
├─ Already registered
│  ├─ Bad faith / your earlier rights, within 5 years
│  │  └─ INVALIDATION (无效宣告)
│  ├─ Mark unused in China for 3 consecutive years
│  │  └─ NON-USE CANCELLATION (撤三)
│  └─ Absolute grounds (deceptive, prohibited signs)
│     └─ INVALIDATION — no 5-year limit
└─ Mark valid AND in use
   └─ Negotiate assignment, or coexistence/license agreement
```

## 1. Opposition (商标异议)

**Window**: within **3 months** of publication in the Trademark Gazette (商标公告). After that, opposition is closed — you must use invalidation.

**Grounds commonly used by foreign brands**:
- Identical/similar to your earlier-filed or well-known mark on identical/similar goods
- Bad-faith squatting under Article 4 / Article 33 Trademark Law (no intent to use, hoarding)
- The applied mark infringes your prior rights (name rights, copyright in logo, portrait)

**Evidence package**:
1. Your earlier trademark certificates (home country + international/Madrid)
2. Evidence of brand fame: sales figures, advertising spend, media coverage, awards (translated)
3. Evidence of the squatter's bad faith: portfolio of hoarded marks, no business matching the class, prior squatting history
4. Chain of title documents (with Chinese translation)

**Process & timeline**:
- File with CNIPA Trademark Office → respondent gets 30 days to respond → examination → decision (~12 months total)
- Losing side may appeal to CNIPA within 15 days

**Official fee**: ¥0 for filing opposition (agency/professional fees separate).

## 2. Invalidation (无效宣告)

**When**: after registration. Relative grounds within **5 years** of registration; bad-faith filings against well-known marks and absolute grounds have **no time limit**.

**Grounds**:
- Bad-faith registration without intent to use (Article 4)
- Conflict with earlier rights / well-known marks
- Absolute grounds: marks lacking distinctiveness, deceptive, against public interest

**Evidence**: same structure as opposition, plus proof that the registered mark coexists with your active business and causes confusion.

**Process**: file with CNIPA → respondent response → examination (often with oral hearing) → decision (~12–18 months). Appeal to Beijing IP Court possible.

**Official fee**: ¥0–500 depending on category; professional fees are the main cost.

## 3. Non-use cancellation (撤三 / 撤销连续三年不使用注册商标)

**When**: anyone can request cancellation of a mark that has not been **genuinely used** in China for **3 consecutive years** after registration.

**Burden of proof reversal**: once you file, the **registrant must prove use** within the period. Most squatters cannot.

**What counts as genuine use** (registrant's evidence):
- Products/packaging with the mark sold in China (invoices, contracts, logistics)
- Advertising, exhibitions, e-commerce listings (Tmall/Taobao/JD records)
- License agreements + actual use by licensee
- Export use with the mark may count in limited cases

**What does NOT count**: internal documents only, symbolic token sales, use in a different form than registered.

**Process**: file with CNIPA → registrant given 2 months to submit use evidence → decision (~9–12 months).

**Strategic note**: 撤三 is often combined with your own new application — file cancellation + immediately file your mark, so the moment the squatter's mark dies, yours is already in the pipeline.

## Cost comparison (typical professional fees, indicative)

| Route | Time pressure | Relative cost | Win driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opposition | 3-month deadline | Low–Medium | Fame + bad-faith evidence |
| Invalidation | 5-year window | Medium–High | Prior rights + bad faith |
| 撤三 | Any time after 3 years unused | Low–Medium | Registrant cannot prove use |

## After winning

- The squatter's mark is refused/cancelled; your own application can proceed.
- Monitor the Gazette for repeat filings by the same party.
- Consider customs recordal + platform enrollment once your mark registers.
