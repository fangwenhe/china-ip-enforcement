# Amazon Brand Registry China — Full Eligibility Checklist

> Standalone checklist for cross-border sellers using a **China-issued trademark** (national CNIPA registration or Madrid designation of China) to enroll in Amazon Brand Registry. Based on Amazon's published Brand Registry requirements.

## Eligibility — the two hard requirements

1. **Permanent brand affixation**: your brand name (word mark) or logo containing the brand name is permanently printed/engraved on products or packaging — not a sticker or handwritten label.
2. **Qualifying trademark**: you are the owner of an **active registered mark (®)** or a **pending application (™)** issued by an IP office Amazon recognizes for that store.

## Accepted mark types

- Word mark (text-based brand name) — simplest
- Design/image mark containing words, letters or numbers
- Pure image marks without text are NOT accepted in most stores

## China-specific document preparation

### For Chinese mainland company applicants
- [ ] Business license (营业执照), unified social credit code (统一社会信用代码)
- [ ] Trademark registration number exactly as issued by CNIPA
- [ ] Applicant company name matches the trademark owner name (Chinese or pinyin as required by the form)
- [ ] Registered address information consistent with the license

### For Hong Kong company applicants
- [ ] HK Business Registration certificate, HK registration number
- [ ] Trademark owner name matches HK registration

### For foreign-company holders of a China mark
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation + Chinese/English name as recorded at CNIPA
- [ ] If the China mark is owned by your Chinese entity but the Amazon store is under the foreign entity, prepare an authorization/relationship document

## Enrollment steps

1. Log into Seller Central → Brands → **Brand Registry**
2. Enroll a new brand: enter brand name **exactly** as registered
3. Enter trademark registration/application number; select issuing office (CNIPA for China national marks)
4. Fill product categories and whether the brand has a registered logo
5. Upload product/packaging images showing the permanently affixed brand
6. Amazon verifies with the trademark office; a verification code may be sent to the trademark contact on file — **make sure the email/phone recorded at CNIPA is monitored**
7. Approval unlocks: A+ Content, Sponsored Brands, Brand Analytics, Project Zero (eligible brands), automated counterfeit scanning

## Common rejection causes

- Brand name on packaging doesn't exactly match the registration
- Sticker labels instead of permanent printing
- Applicant is not the trademark owner (distributors cannot enroll without owner authorization)
- Trademark contact email at CNIPA is stale — verification code never received
- Design mark submitted but products show only text (or vice versa)

## Pending (TM) vs registered (R) marks

- Pending applications are accepted, with **reduced protection tools** until registration completes
- Once registered, update the Brand Registry status to unlock the full toolkit
- Amazon IP Accelerator is a separate paid route — not needed if you already have a CNIPA filing

## After enrollment — enforcement tools

- **Report a Violation (RAV)**: submit takedowns for trademark/copyright/patent infringement
- Automated protection: Amazon scans listings 24/7 against your mark
- Transparency / Project Zero (by invitation/eligibility): serialized anti-counterfeit codes
