> Bilingual template for IP infringement complaints on Chinese platforms (Tmall/Taobao, Douyin, Pinduoduo, JD, 1688). Informational template — adapt to each platform's portal fields and attach your certificate scans.

## C. Evidence checklist (attach to every complaint)

- [ ] Registration certificate (trademark/copyright) — clear scan
- [ ] Identity/business license of rights holder (+ Chinese translation if foreign)
- [ ] Power of attorney if filed by an agent
- [ ] Screenshots of infringing listing (URL, seller name, date visible)
- [ ] Comparison table: your mark/work vs infringing content
- [ ] Purchase evidence (test buy) for counterfeit goods claims
- [ ] For repeat infringers: history of prior removed links

## Tips

- File **one complaint per link batch**, keep ticket numbers; escalate unresolved tickets after 7 days.
- Copyright certificates work for stolen images/photos/listing copy; trademarks work for brand misuse; combine both when possible.
- Keep a weekly enforcement log; persistent repeat sellers may justify administrative complaint or civil action.
