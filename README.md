## Why enforcement matters

- China is **first-to-file**: if a squatter registered your brand first, you must actively attack — passive waiting lets the registration solidify.
- Cross-border sellers lose the most: copied listings, hijacked store names, counterfeit goods — and Amazon Brand Registry rejects you without a valid trademark.
- **The cheapest win is early**: oppose during the 3-month publication window; full invalidation or litigation costs 5–20× more.

## Trademark opposition (异议)

- **When**: a third party files an opposition within **3 months of your (or someone's) mark publication** in the CNIPA Trademark Gazette.
- **Who wins**: you must show earlier rights (home registration, prior use evidence, fame) or bad-faith squatting evidence.
- **Timeline**: ~12 months from filing to decision.
- **Key tip**: watch the Gazette weekly for your brand class — most foreign brands only find out years later.

## Invalidation & non-use cancellation (无效宣告 / 撤三)

- **Invalidation (无效宣告)**: challenge a registered mark that should not have been approved (bad faith, earlier rights, deceptive registration). Available any time for absolute grounds; relative grounds within 5 years.
- **Non-use cancellation (撤三 / 撤销连续三年不使用)**: if a registered mark has not been used in China for **3 consecutive years**, anyone can request cancellation — often the cheapest way to remove a squatted mark.
- **Evidence matters**: keep your registration, invoices, export records, store listings — they become your proof of use or the squatter's lack of use.

## Platform takedowns: Tmall / Taobao / Douyin / TikTok

| Platform | IP complaint portal | Typical speed |
|---|---|---|
| Tmall / Taobao | Alibaba IP Protection Platform (ipp.alibabagroup.com) | 3–7 days for verified marks |
| Douyin / TikTok (China) | Douyin IP protection platform | 5–10 days |
| Pinduoduo / JD | Brand protection portal | 7–14 days |
| Amazon (cross-border) | Amazon Brand Registry → Report a violation | 1–7 days once enrolled |

- **You need**: a registered China trademark (or copyright certificate for images/content) + product/listing evidence.
- **Habit**: send one complaint per infringement link; repeat weekly until the pattern stops.

## Amazon Brand Registry China checklist

Before enrolling your brand (or a China-mark-backed brand) in Amazon Brand Registry:

- [ ] You own an **active registered trademark (®)** or **pending application (™)** in each country where you enroll
- [ ] The mark matches your brand name **exactly** on your products/packaging (permanently affixed)
- [ ] You are the trademark **owner** (authorized agents cannot enroll on your behalf without proof)
- [ ] Registration country matches your Amazon store (for China-registered marks, the China/HK store context)
- [ ] Company details match: for Chinese mainland companies, the unified social credit code; for HK companies, the HK registration number
- [ ] Product images show the brand name permanently printed (not stickers/hand-written)

> **China-specific note**: a China national trademark or Madrid-designated-China registration is what anchors brand protection for sellers doing cross-border China business. A US/EU trademark does **not** protect you inside China's e-commerce ecosystem.

## Customs recordal

- Record your trademark/copyright with **China Customs IP Protection** (中国海关知识产权备案).
- Effect: counterfeit goods at ports are automatically detained and reported to you — works both ways (goods made in China going out, and fakes coming in).
- Valid for 10 years, renewable.

## FAQ

**Q: Someone already registered my brand — can I still win it back?**
A: Usually yes — by opposition (if published), invalidation (if registered in bad faith), or 撤三 (if unused 3 years). The earlier you act, the cheaper.

**Q: Does Amazon accept a pending (TM) trademark?**
A: Yes — Amazon Brand Registry accepts pending applications from recognized offices (including China), with fewer protection tools than a registered mark.

**Q: Should I police every infringement myself?**
A: No — prioritize revenue-impacting listings first. Use automated platform tools after Brand Registry enrollment.
